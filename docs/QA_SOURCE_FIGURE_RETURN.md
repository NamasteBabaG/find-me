# Source-qualified whole-figure return

`source-figure-return/v1` is a finite, free composition from an already settled
painting. It is neither a new painting nor permission to approve an image.
It is restricted to QA worlds with a still valid original completion grant.
The normal new-game four-dollar grant alone does not enable it.

The additive public authority in
`content/detectives/kingdom/figure-return-v1.ts` names one exact source,
placement, original parent and active baseline. The historical authored return,
shrink-only diagnosis, raw response, request key and composition are preserved.
Two source-qualified additions restore the crown and lower body together.
Protected source cores form holes in those additions. Complete original head
bounds are recorded separately from those cores: only the specifically named
lower loose hair may be naturally occluded. A preserved core is not proof that
an entire neighboring person is correct.

Before a write, the service revalidates ownership, verified payment, original
source and identity, the immutable completion grant, settled RAW, exact paid
diagnosis and baseline recipe. It reproduces the baseline geometry with the
historical join's shared function. It refuses an existing question, including
an orphaned reservation/settlement without a recorded round. Recorded rounds
and reconstructed question keys are checked under the same lease, game and
ledger fence as the write. No question or answer is rewritten.

The compositor works at native crop size. The new joins keep the existing
24-point seam limit and zero-shift requirement. Source cores and pixels outside
the additions must remain unchanged, and the old source lower-body region must
be replaced completely by the retained RAW. It does not invent a silhouette,
resample the child, apply blur or transfer any visual approval.

The new geometry uses a closed source-bound measurement region including the
crown and boots. Historical geometry and normal runs are unchanged. A marker
binds the original receipt, paid RAW, source, identity, new pixels and new tap
geometry. Publication reproduces all of them; copied audit zeros, a bare marker
or a mutable PASS are insufficient. The audit is purged on deletion, alongside
the baseline/new game images and retained private evidence. Financial history
and full UNKNOWN reserves survive deletion.

One free correction yields its worker slice before any provider dispatch. The
new shipping then requires a fresh ordinary MEDIUM assessment of all quality,
identity, source-neighbor, complete-head, body, pose, scale, seams, style and
doubt checks. Its native context includes every additional source head. The
new prompt distinguishes the compound permission from a rectangular editable
window and explicitly checks sharpness, edge softness and local detail against
nearby people at the same depth. More contrast, less blur or a complete crown
alone never proves style or whole-picture acceptance.

Local mocked tests, a deployment, a free offline prototype and a genuine paid
assessment have different evidential scope. This feature alone is no claim of
a finished original game or a complete new 27-hide game within four dollars.
