# Bounded recovery after unanswered single reviews

This release changes recovery and candidate admission. It does not approve a
picture, increase a budget, erase billing history or qualify a complete game at
the four-dollar ceiling.

`single-transport-continuation/v1` admits at most one new question about the same
pixels after an engine-cancelled deadline or a non-permanent provider failure.
The original round must be settled and unusable with no answer text. The existing
purchase ledger reserves the complete continuation under its own request key;
UNKNOWN and unpriced outcomes retain the separate reserved-interruption route.
Account, policy and invalid-request failures do not qualify.

New combined MEDIUM Flex background intents record
`single-medium-flex-wait/v1` and allow 25 minutes before cancellation. Stored
intents without this marker keep their original deadline and bytes. LOW and
historical HIGH policies are unchanged.

An appearance whose evidence route is exhausted no longer blocks another hide's
finite final painting. A painter's conclusive `gave-up` result is recorded as an
unroutable rest only in automatic self-repair worlds. Historical worlds retain
their end-of-world third attempt. Parking records structured per-hide diagnostics without changing
the historical evidence-only summary. A new source-body painting requires room
for both the image and a complete bounded assessment before it reserves or
dispatches; paid request replay and already generated rows do not buy again.

`crown-tab-continuity-repair/v1` is a prospective, nested candidate marker for one
narrow case: authenticated paid evidence locates a complete RAW crown above an
authorized return, shipping cuts that crown, the quality faults are crown-only,
and continuity reports one connected visible person with one hard cut. An
explicit bystander refusal, another fault or a failed subject check is refused.
The quality stage itself is checked even when the combined row verdict includes
the continuity refusal. Older crown-tab candidates retain their exact-match
quality and passing-continuity requirements.

The candidate is re-derived from the paid RAW, unchanged source and original
shipping. The existing source authority, neighbor protection, seam limit,
ownership, identity, payment and immutable answer checks remain mandatory. Its
new receipt is pending and contains no inherited approval. A fresh ordinary
review must pass before publication. The old receipt, pixels, attempts and
ledger snapshot are preserved, and publication revalidates the new marker.

Qualification requires the complete repository gate on the frozen tree, the
exact pushed commit's seven CI jobs including `verify`, build/privacy audits and
QA-only deployment with served health and SHA checks. Synthetic tests and free
offline replay of retained evidence establish technical behavior, not live
provider acceptance or complete-game cost qualification.
