# Standing five-dollar emergency ceiling for the v12 QA engine

The owner explicitly approved a five-dollar ceiling per world on 2026-09-30: `תאשר 5 לעולם / יש אישור`. A paid v12 world that hits the four-dollar reservation limit now receives its one-dollar emergency allowance automatically, without another parent approval.

The ordinary four-dollar ceiling remains the starting limit. The first real transactional refusal must describe a new purchase that fits the inclusive five-dollar ceiling. The world must be paid, owned, unpublished, undeleted, use all nine v12 boards, and have no refund, pending purchase, conflicting bill, overrun or unapproved UNKNOWN hold. Activation appends a single immutable SYSTEM policy receipt and advances the ledger revision under the game and job fences. It preserves the ledger snapshot, every retained UNKNOWN reservation, assets and quality approvals.

The normal purchase guard and creation status endpoint read the same verified receipt. The next pipeline tick can reserve within five dollars; the progress screen also reports that ceiling and continues polling. The grant cannot stack or enable a purchase above five dollars. It is restricted to the current v12 QA local-patch engine and durable DB storage; historical engines and production enrollment retain their existing guards.

The allowance is financial permission only. It neither approves an image nor publishes a partial game. Publication still requires all 27 actual hide approvals. A finite ceiling cannot guarantee that arbitrary provider failures or repeated bad images will always finish; a world that cannot reserve within five dollars remains subject to its existing budget guard. The global daily spend guard is also unchanged.
