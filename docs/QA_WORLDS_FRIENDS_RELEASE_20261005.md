## Resulting experience

1. **Owned world selection.** The owner map has a persistent world selector even for a one-world game. Ready worlds resume their own progress; preparing, payment-pending and unavailable states remain distinct. New-world purchase opens a separate parent flow. Guests cannot enter that selector or the parent's other worlds. Catalog/navigation uses neutral world emblems rather than revealing unreached boards.

3. **Continuation for the same child.** Parent confirmation creates or resumes one server-owned child/world intent, preserves the resumed age and returns to the original context after sign-in. The new rendering profile cannot alter an older game's frozen profile. A new world currently requires a new photo because approved identity is bound to the selected world/style atlas. The server prices the first world at39 ILS and subsequent worlds at30 ILS, under a child-wide payment fence. Every package tier obeys that fence and rejects a package containing an already-paid complete world before another charge. Pending ordinary multi-world drafts retain their original checkout path.

4. **Independent friends.** Parent-authorized invitations share one frozen generated world for30 days. Guests choose an optional predefined nickname without an account, free-text name, email, age or photo. Their visits, finds, hints, discoveries and final preset reaction are independent from the owner's progress. The owner can distinguish unvisited, visited with zero finds, partial and complete boards. Identical nicknames are separate participants. Only report cards actually shown acknowledge their captured activity revision. Reports remain90 days after the earlier closure/expiry boundary; replacement retains old results privately. Revoked, expired or repaired/stale invitations cannot remain active.

## Recovery and UX

- Starting another player first drains acknowledged progress and performs a fresh inspection, then opens nickname selection directly. Save/inspection failure keeps the current game and identity. Back remains available after failure. An ambiguous join preserves its exact key and attempted nickname across Back/retry.
- Another tab switching this invitation's participant stops the old tab through a non-authorizing signal and authoritative server/cookie checks. Local progress/outboxes are scoped to both invitation and participant. A malformed cache row does not erase valid pending events; server validation stays strict. Repeated permanent rejection stops with explicit reopen feedback.
- Every new child control uses64px touch targets. Press, pending, disabled, offline and recovery states remain visible. Dialog focus/Escape, HE/EN, RTL and reduced motion are retained. Guest gameplay occupies the viewport remaining below the participant strip, without changing owner hit geometry.
- An abandoned checkout is closed only after the provider confirms terminal nonpayment. Expired leases and FAILED/unknown responses alone do not unlock another payment. A saved FAILED checkout can retry its exact original session. Historical/deleted games have an owner financial recovery route without restoring art. Mock cancellation prevents payment from an old mock tab.
- Return paths are validated after URL normalization. Mock destinations are generated from stored order/proof context, never caller-supplied URLs. Order display requires owner/admin/creator-draft authority. Photo completion uses an exact claim, so a late earlier upload cannot replace the profile after a newer checkout began.
- Public privacy/terms and parent consent now disclose the displayed child's name/illustrated character, predefined guest nickname, independent results and retention. Existing business/refund/statutory wording and legal version are unchanged.

## Database rollout and evidence limits

The additive migration was applied only to the verified QA schema. The25 existing games' content fingerprint was unchanged immediately across that migration. Three new tables have RLS enabled and no PUBLIC/anon/authenticated client grants. Actual PostgreSQL metadata passed the strict assertion:33 columns,12 valid indexes,3 primary keys and5 foreign keys. No existing game config, image or engine setting was changed by the migration.

Local browser checks use a disposable SQLite fixture and synthetic/public art, generation off, mock payment and console email. They establish native magic-link sign-in, one/two-world navigation, continuation price/age/context, independent guest visits/results, and payment closure/resume. Hebrew guest controls were inspected at 320×640, 390×640 and 640×360. A clipped landscape collection close button was repaired by sizing the collection against the game container below the identity strip; the close button is now fully visible and operable. Collection selection/return, Back to map, saved-player switching and returning to the previous player's resume choice were exercised. These checks do not prove a new image's quality, phone back gestures, child usability or concurrent Prisma behavior on PostgreSQL.
