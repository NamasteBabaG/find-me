## Runtime change

New strict local-patch rows enroll in `local-patch-adaptive-recovery/v1`.
Completed failures are archived by attempt, including a digest of the judgement
and rejected-pixel evidence. Before the normal third/final attempt, the worker
combines both failures and freezes a diagnosis bound to the row, identity, age,
art, render policy and authored hide. The final prompt addresses the diagnosed
placement, registration, identity, age/scale or paint defect. Only typed check
codes enter instructions; reviewer prose is not executable guidance.

The frozen plan participates in the paid-operation fingerprint and survives a
lost worker write. Historical rows without enrollment retain their old recipe.
Unreadable evidence is held for reconciliation instead of spending on another
unrelated image. This is bounded diagnosis and changed rendering instructions,
not a promise that every provider response can be fixed or accepted.

The old single-hide prompt requests `brief reason` while its parser expected
`reason`. The parser now accepts that exact alias, rejects conflicting aliases,
and preserves every quality check. Retained age-aware verdicts use their actual
versioned schema. No prompt or existing paid fingerprint changes for this fix.
