# Recover an unanswered review of a source figure

A deterministic `source-figure-return/v1` composition remains the same image
when an unanswered ordinary assessment marks its row FAILED. The composition
verifier is also used when preparing its admitted transport continuation. Its
old GENERATED-only guard therefore prevented that continuation before dispatch.

Verification now accepts FAILED only while reauthenticating an existing derived
image. Creation still requires GENERATED and its unreviewed original recipe.
All audit, identity, payment, ownership, source, RAW, image, geometry, counter,
cost and exact deterministic-reproduction checks remain in force. No image,
receipt, old question, attempt or budget is changed by this verification.

The existing transport policy alone decides whether a new question is allowed:
one default-tier MEDIUM continuation after an authenticated settled deadline
cancellation or transient provider failure without text. A real visual refusal,
readable answer, permanent failure, unverified charge or a spent continuation
does not gain another opinion. Full UNKNOWN reservations keep their own route.
Composition provenance is not publication or a transferred visual approval.

Native regressions exercise both a cancelled first assessment and a failed one,
then the fresh ordinary assessment, unchanged pixels/attempts/costs, no new
painting/diagnosis and no third question. Historical source-figure cases,
orphan reservations, full reserves, tamper checks and deletion remain covered.
