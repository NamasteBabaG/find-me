## Scene direction

A friendly inhabited castle entrance during preparations for a small festival:
banner sewing, bread delivery, a puppet rehearsal, wooden-toy repair, gardeners,
a sleepy small dragon, children chalking the paving, a messenger sorting letters,
musicians tuning instruments, a balcony storyteller and market customers.
Aim for roughly 55–70 readable people in 15–20 small interactions, distributed
over the frame. These are compositional targets, not a reason to sacrifice faces
or anatomy. Keep little empty paving/sky and avoid filler flowers/sparkles.
