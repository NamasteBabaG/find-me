## Visual inspection

The dry dome, broad sandy floor and large architecture are gone. Blue and turquoise water runs between reef clusters. A large blue whale fills the upper-middle area; two large friendly octopuses, orange left and violet right, anchor music and gift-wrapping stories. Coral is abundant, including pink, purple, orange and yellow clusters. Other visible stories include a turtle mail carrier, bubble-ring swimmers, toy-submarine play, mermaid storytelling, shell collecting, fish sketching, reef gardening, crab shell stacking, a small octopus in a cup-shaped shell and manta-ray photography. Sunlight from upper left and cool reef shadows establish underwater light.

The six native crops inspected include the two submarine children, octopus music scene, mask, pearl, magnifier and pendant. Their shapes, hands and coral details are legible; no broad smearing is visible in these samples. The image remains textured. Faces skew more cartoon-like/large-eyed than the approved castle crop, so do not claim an exact style match. Participants are mostly children/merfolk, and fewer than the requested 45-50; the enormous creatures occupy substantial area. Density and face-style acceptance remain with the user.

Clear bubble helmets keep human faces readable, but some hair visibly flows outside their rims. This is a fantasy convention, not a validated physically sealed helmet. Do not claim perfect helmet geometry; this needs attention before personal-child placement. No personal identity is rendered here.
