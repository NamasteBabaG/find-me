# Finite Journey SOURCE-head recovery v2

`source-head-recovery/v2` is a separate, prospective recovery recipe for two
version-13 Journey placements: New York 1 and Paris 1. It does not alter the
version-12 Explorer recovery, historical paintings, questions, or answers.

The public authority is `content/detectives/journey-source-head-v2.ts`. It pins
the encoded SOURCE, decoded SOURCE PNG, active crop, child envelope, return
window, original target head, protected people, and permitted head footprint.
Paris uses its active body-route window. Another person's feet overlap the
padded target-head registration there; they remain protected SOURCE pixels.
Registration rectangles are role geometry, not generated-hair segmentation.

The painter receives the original native 512 by 768 SOURCE crop, canonical
identity, approved original photograph, and the closed head-only mask. Only a
new head, hairstyle, face, and neck may change. SOURCE body, clothing, hands,
props, continuing people, and background remain exact. Composition copies paid
head pixels at their original axes without resampling, head fading, sharpening,
or translation. The unchanged exterior threshold is 24. A diagnostic RAW
background shift never moves the published head or SOURCE body. The full
SOURCE body remains tappable even though only its head changes.

Provider encoding is a separate step before those native-copy guarantees.
`journey-head-provider-frame/v1` admits the original 512x768 response and the
observed 768x1152 response, both with exactly the same aspect ratio. The latter
receives the existing single full-frame Lanczos3 normalization to 512x768;
there is no crop or aspect-ratio stretch. Every other frame fails closed.
The original bytes and bill remain immutable. All native head-pixel comparisons
refer to that normalized RAW, with no subsequent head resampling or enhancement.
Renderer, retained-RAW reader, reviewer and publication use the same mapping.

An earlier release stopped a settled 768x1152 head before normalization. The
explicit QA-only `journey-head-frame-v1` restore recovers precisely that failure
once. It requires the original head pin, settled retained RAW, exact failed
receipt, unchanged 26 siblings, payment, owner, original identity/photo and
full ledger. It composes a new unapproved GAME asset without buying anything,
incrementing an attempt or counting the same cost twice. The private audit
preserves the failed row, all request hashes and both RAW hashes. A normalized
replay asset cannot publish with its frame receipt missing or altered. Deletion
clears that duplicated private audit as well as the original retained files.

Enrollment is a free, named QA operation. It requires the verified payment,
owner, original identity/photo/crop, original MEDIUM birth authority, all 27
rows, a concluded failed first attempt, its settled retained painting, and an
unused second-painting key. The immutable private pin retains the original
question, row, asset, all historical request hashes, and all 26 sibling hashes.
It changes no old pixels or bills and transfers no approval. The next head
cannot enroll until the previous head has an ordinary publication-qualified
pass.

There is one additional image key per enrolled placement: `render:2`. Lost
checkpoints resume that key and its retained RAW. They do not buy a third image.
Once a Journey head pin exists, ordinary image/diagnosis scheduling stays scoped
to enrolled head candidates; an unrelated failed placement cannot consume the
remaining budget in the same or a later slice.

The existing four-dollar ledger remains authoritative. New-image admission
requires the full image reservation plus 178,000 microUSD of prospective review
coverage. Native preparation tests cover both complete Flex and Default
questions, including every evidence image and all 6,000 output tokens. Runtime
checks the actual final prepared bound against that coverage. Each question
still reserves its own exact full standard-rate maximum; the allowance is not
an average bill, Flex discount, or replacement reservation. Every existing
UNKNOWN remains reserved in full. Inadequate capacity or a new unresolved bill
stops further purchases. No cap extension, grant transfer, or history reset is
part of this recipe.

All quality, identity, age, source-person, anatomy, native detail, head/hair, and
join checks remain in one fresh ordinary MEDIUM assessment. A stated age of
seven still fails a genuinely estimated range of seven to nine. Public mask
geometry cannot approve a clipped hairstyle, an old hair remnant, a blurred or
oversharp face, or a broken neck. Repeated poses or SOURCE-preserved clothing
do not by themselves prove a defect.

Publication re-reads payment, identity, the pin, the settled RAW and complete
bill, the actual GAME asset, native composition, geometry, and ordinary review.
Assets with the `ast_jnh_` prefix cannot fall through to a legacy receipt if
their new marker is absent. Deletion clears the private pin's duplicated
assessment/photo references and every retained image/reply while preserving
the separate accounting ledger.

Mock pipeline tests and a green gate qualify implementation only. They do not
prove artistic likeness, natural head/neck joins, provider latency, a complete
real game, or completion under four dollars. Those claims require the separate
authorized QA run and its retained actual images and full ledger.
