and body/prop areas that remain closed. It applies only at the source body's

counter, past question, answer or ledger request. The next ordinary slice may

head/hair/neck mask. The service composes new paid pixels only within that

transplanted. The fixed head boundary retains the existing24 difference and

The new painter recipe keeps the original source head angle and task-directed
gaze. The historical subject plan and its identity/age binding stay stored and

The native regression uses synthetic identities, drawings, diagnoses and

# Additive paid-RAW join recovery

`source-head-join/v2` is a separate, free composition of the already-settled
fourth head painting. It does not change the v1 prompt, mask, image fingerprint,
paid envelope, original head authority, counter, attempt limit or old failure.
The original `composeSourceHead` implementation remains unchanged.

The v1 interior ring can contain the hair that was explicitly requested to
change. Comparing that hair against the old source colour can reject a valid
replacement. V2 measures the same 24 limit on the protected exterior instead.
It copies the exact RAW head at native axes, without translation, resizing or
feathering. All pixels outside the existing public mask come from SOURCE,
including body, hands, spoon, counter and other heads. RAW-only background
registration is measured and recorded separately; it is not applied to the
head or interpreted as an output-body shift. The unchanged outer-return guard
still checks the actual assembled output.

Only a failed, unreviewed fourth attempt with this exact v1 join refusal can
receive the one-time SYSTEM replay pin. It requires the original completion
grant, unchanged 26 siblings and original purchases/UNKNOWN reserves, a paid
unrefunded game and exact settled retained fourth RAW. It saves the complete
old failed receipt and binds the new candidate, original source authority,
retained payload and operation fingerprint. A missing retained image cannot
trigger a fifth painting; the replay renderer refuses any provider dispatch.
The replay pins the post-settlement row invariant, including the already
recorded failed painting cost, and adds no second image cost to that row.
Revoked completion capacity or a refund refuses the wake before any replay.
Publication independently reproduces the output and verifies both immutable
pins and the actual asset bytes. No old review is transferred: a new ordinary
MEDIUM assessment must establish complete hair/head, identity, age, anatomy,
source people, style, sharpness and both internal and outer joins.

The real first v1 trial was refused locally before a paid assessment. That
refusal is evidence for investigating the metric, not proof that its artwork
passes. No supplier call, test pass or preservation proof alone establishes
