# Collection recovery after two failures

The current nine-board, 27-hide collection now treats a concluded visual failure
as work for its own queue. It does not fail the game after the third render or
ask the parent to approve an incomplete/failed picture. Legacy pinned v6-v9
purchase recipes remain unchanged.

After two failed normal attempts (or an unreadable completed quality review),
the queue freezes a recovery phase before making another purchase. A Sol vision
diagnosis compares the original crop, approved canonical portrait, up to four
authenticated retained raw images and the failed shipping composition. Its
strictly validated plan identifies the cause and chooses either recomposition
of an existing paid picture or a redraw with a different editable envelope.
The original crop, board, pose, support, identity and 27-target count stay fixed.

The plan can move the return window within the original crop to restore a head
or foot that the compositor clipped. The face and whole visible child have an
18px protected margin; no sibling crop can overlap. Only background pixels in
the six-pixel blend band are blended. The child is never stretched or warped.
Diagnostic prose is recorded as evidence and never inserted into the painter's
instructions. Only typed causes and validated geometry control the next action.

An independent Sol board review assesses the actual delivered pixels, the
corrected native face region, canonical identity, age, scale and seams. Existing
successful siblings keep their original approvals and pixels. The diagnosis
cannot approve its own image. A new visual refusal returns to diagnosis with
the failed recipe recorded; changing explanatory prose or an unused redraw
source key does not count as a different approach. Identical rejected pixels
cannot buy another visual verdict (unreadable wire evidence is distinguished
from a genuine visual refusal).

Every phase, model call and recovery image has a durable key/fingerprint in the
same world ledger. Lost diagnosis/image writes resume the retained paid reply
without repurchasing. Deletion inventories include these new keys before their
dispatch. Identity, payment, game lifecycle, target state and worker lease are
rechecked at commit. A historical terminal v10 quality refusal can rejoin this
queue automatically, preserving the original rows and accounting.

This is autonomous quality recovery, not unlimited spending or a claim that
providers cannot fail. The existing per-world ceiling, kill switch and unknown
charge barriers still apply. A capacity exhaustion stays in generation and
backs off for an hour with an operational job diagnostic; it cannot monopolize
the cron queue or create a parent quality-approval task. It requires capacity
to become available before paid work can continue. No failed picture is silently
published to conceal an infrastructure or accounting problem.

Verification covers real SQLite queue/ledger/retained-store/publication flows:
27-hide delivery after an additional rejected repair, unchanged sibling images
and approvals, restart after a retained diagnosis, restart after a paid redraw,
changed render-mask pixels, preserved attempt counters, deletion inventory and
budget backoff without extra dispatch. Network calls are synthetic in tests;
they are not evidence of approval of any live child's game.
