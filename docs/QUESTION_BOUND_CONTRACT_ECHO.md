# Question-bound contract echo recovery

A completed, paid single MEDIUM assessment can report every visual role yet
remain unreadable because the model mistyped the redundant contract digest.
The malformed digest is not evidence of damaged artwork and does not justify a
new painting or another opinion about the same pixels.

`source-neighbor-envelope/v3-question-bound-contract` is a separately named
reading. It admits only a hexadecimal contract echo of 1–128 characters whose
length is not 64. The original strict bundle reader must locate that exact
shape fault. A complete, validated expected binding supplies the authoritative
digest; the complete original source contract is independently revalidated.
The original candidate echo, source IDs, mappings, geometry, observations and
all quality, subject, continuity and visible-head checks remain strict.

A 64-character conflicting digest remains a conflict. Missing or non-hex
metadata, a malformed candidate echo, altered real source contracts, foreign or
missing source IDs and invalid geometry remain unresolved. Neighbor damage or
uncertainty remains damage or uncertainty. The original provider bytes are
never replaced, persisted as a new answer or silently relabeled.

The service authenticates the original dispatched question body and complete
image/geometry/identity binding, terminal completed provider round, retained
payload and matching settled bill before recording a versioned reading marker.
Publication repeats that authentication. The reader itself grants no purchase,
payment, budget extension, image approval or production-release authority.
Its live authentication remains QA-only.

The candidate-only v2 module and historical strict reader are unchanged.
Existing v2 markers retain their own reading. The new marker is selected only
for its own malformed contract-echo case; all three paid roles share that same
authenticated marker, and the prior failed receipt remains in review history.
No image, attempt, original question, answer, bill, full UNKNOWN reserve or
accepted sibling is reset or discarded.

Validation uses synthetic fixtures. Domain controls cover valid conflicting
digests, metadata shape, source IDs, geometry and semantic faults. The native
world regression verifies free adoption, publication, refusal on altered
image/geometry/identity/variant/question/policy/terminal/bill evidence, original
payload preservation, full UNKNOWN preservation and zero new provider calls.
