SCENE / COMPOSITION — THE MOST IMPORTANT CONSTRAINT:
A joyful, extraordinarily living Amazon discovery lagoon, beneath enormous buttress-root trees, tangled lianas and a richly varied canopy. A close transverse turquoise-green stream separates a wildlife bank from a busy raised observation trail. Roots, low logs, leafy alcoves and a modest safe wooden boardwalk weave together small human stories and animal habitats. Show much more life than empty walkway or water. No huge blank central lake, no vista disappearing to a vanishing point, no long receding pier, no tiny background expedition.
Slightly elevated side-on view, gently zoomed out to fit about 40-45 people, but with SHALLOW COMPRESSED DEPTH: three closely overlapping lateral bands of activities, like an expansive living illustration stage. Background is foliage and softly veiled trunks, NOT distant small humans. All human groups belong to essentially the same legible scale. Adult full-height equivalent about 15-18% of image height, children about 10-13%; sitting changes pose, not head/hand scale. Child heads remain approximately the size of neighboring adult heads, not half-size. This rule is especially critical in the LOWER LEFT: its children, seated parents, hands, faces and equipment must be as legible and similarly sized as adjacent central/foreground groups. No giant cropped person at bottom, no miniature family tucked into a hollow root. No collage of differently scaled vignettes.

ABUNDANT LIFE, LAYERED THROUGH THE FRAME:
Let children keep discovering animals within plausible foliage and roots rather than lining all animals up like zoo displays. A beautifully patterned jaguar resting with a playful cub on the separate wildlife bank, partly framed by leaves but faces and rosettes readable; a relaxed caiman in water by that bank; a green boa coiled over a low branch and a second patterned snake partly behind distant-bank roots, away from people. Sloth with baby above a human-scale group, small monkeys with distinct poses and wrapping tails, capybara family near reeds, giant river otters playing in the stream, turtles on a fallen log, a green iguana, scarlet macaws, toucans peering through leaves and small hummingbirds at heliconia. Leafcutter ants carrying leaves across a root, a well-camouflaged leaf insect, beetles, dragonflies and small yellow/brown butterflies. These are intimate discoverable animal stories, not threatening predator scenes or giant animal portraits. No attacking, riding, petting, feeding or handling wild predators. Guides and children remain on firm safe ground or the railed observation trail; wildlife bank is separated by water. No tiger or African leopard.

HUMAN STORIES, VARIED AND NATURAL:
Crowded with distinct small groups of children, parents and local naturalist guides: comparing a jaguar footprint cast with a boot print; drawing a toucan; measuring an enormous fallen leaf together; looking at a leaf insect with a hand lens; spotting a sloth through a gap in foliage; comparing seed pods and leaf rubbings; assembling a small leaf-and-twig boat in a shallow contained tray; showing younger children how to use a map; loading supplies into a secured canoe (lifejackets at the boat); weaving a small palm-leaf keepsake; comparing animal sketches; a child sheltering playfully under a huge fallen leaf after a sprinkle. One monkey examines a fallen fruit while a guide points it out. Groups stand, sit on broad roots, kneel or lean on railings; not everybody gathered around identical tables. Warm curiosity, humor and cooperation, not everybody looking out at the camera. Human faces, ages, hair, skin tones, profiles and expressions varied; no repeated bearded adult template.

Distinct leaf shapes and visible bark/veins, not tangled scratchy noise. Rich jade, emerald, sage and warm olive greens with localized coral/pink/orange flowers and bright bird accents — no blanket neon saturation. Warm broken sunbeams from openings high left, brighter leaf clearings alternating with gentle cooler green-blue canopy shade. Faces and target objects readable even in shade; light must fall on people and vegetation consistently. Airy morning glow, not a dark green cave, no fog over the search field. Water and wet leaves can glint; dry wood, fabric, skin and fur are mostly matte. Modern lightweight exploration clothes in teal, coral, mustard, pale blue, plum and cream; varied field shirts, shorts/trousers, practical shoes and a few soft hats. Not identical khaki uniforms or imported historical desert costumes.
