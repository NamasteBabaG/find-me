# Question-bound reading of a malformed candidate echo

`source-neighbor-envelope/v2-question-bound-candidate` is a later reading of an
existing paid MEDIUM document. It does not change the v1 source-neighbor reader,
the original question, provider answer, model, price, image or source contract.
An old receipt without the explicit later-reading marker retains its old reader.

The candidate digest echoed by the model is redundant with the dispatched
question's authenticated image binding. A lower-case hexadecimal string of the
wrong length can be read using that question binding only after the service
reconstructs the original question and proves its exact fingerprint and body
digest, variant, image, geometry, identity and authored-return binding. A valid
but different 64-character digest stays a conflict. Missing, non-hexadecimal,
oversized or otherwise malformed evidence stays unresolved. No edit-distance
threshold is used, and no modified answer is stored.

The QA-only service also requires the original completed background round, the
same provider response/request ID and its settled bill, the retained payload's
digest, model, policy and original raw text. Unknown and pending charges cannot
authorize this reading. Existing UNKNOWN money elsewhere in the world stays
reserved in full; adoption never creates capacity or adjusts the ledger.

The new marker is shared by all three roles projected from that one MEDIUM
answer. The ordinary quality, subject, continuity, head and source-correspondence
readers still decide every semantic field. A duplication, remnant, broken body,
identity failure, uncertain source mapping or visible cut cannot become a pass
because its candidate echo can now be read. No new provider opinion is bought.

The free retained-answer route appends the prior decision to `reviewHistory`,
names the new reader and retains the exact provider raw text and fingerprint.
Publication independently re-resolves the source contract and re-authenticates
the complete marker against the dispatched question, terminal response, retained
payload and bill. A copied marker or cached PASS does not authorize publication.

Synthetic regression scenarios exercise both a 55-character and a 65-character
echo with real local ledgers, ordinary mocked background transport and the real
publication path. One leaves all remaining capacity reserved as UNKNOWN; the
other retains enough capacity for a new question. Both leave an unreviewed
sibling ready, then adopt through a `review-existing/v1` world turn and yield
before any sibling question, image or diagnosis purchase. They preserve
attempts, image, costs and exact paid bytes,
and reject tampered bindings, question body, policy, terminal state, retained
payload, provider request ID and inconsistent role markers. These tests and
offline replays are technical evidence; they do not prove live art quality,
complete either original QA game or establish a complete game's four-dollar cost.
