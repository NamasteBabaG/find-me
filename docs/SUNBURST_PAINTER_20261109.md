# Prospective Sunburst painter

Guy selected `gpt-image-2.6-sunburst` on 9 November 2026. The new profile uses
`quality=high`, `complexity=low`, opaque PNG, one image, and the existing
768x1152 edit frame. It keeps the owned photograph, canonical identity, masks,

The initial canonical identity generation has its own historical contract.

## Selection and preservation

`LOCAL_PATCH_PAINTER=sunburst-v1` is QA-only and prospective. A fresh lean draft
gets `local-patch-painter/sunburst-high-low-complexity-v1` after its lean and source
contracts. Both draft entry points use the same transaction. Source families 12
and 13 are supported. The exact receipt and four-dollar lean pin are required
when reading the game. A corrupt pin refuses generation; it never selects a
different model. Unsupported whole-source-context frames refuse before purchase.

The environment never repins paid or started games. Historical LOW/MEDIUM policy
hashes, request settings, stored questions and answers remain unchanged. A retained
purchase keeps its exact original subject direction and fingerprint. Only a new
key buys the new profile. A photo-backed Sunburst call cannot silently revert to
the historical model, and a historical game cannot request Sunburst HIGH.

## Money and limits

The selected full standard rates are 5 micro-USD per text-input token, 8 per
image-input token and 30 per output-image token. No cache or Flex discount is
assumed for painting. These are the published rates, not a measured FindMe price.
The model, explicit quality, complexity and rate card are in the request capture
and policy identity. A different complexity is a different operation.

The new image reservation is 150,000 micro-USD. Before a new image or repair,
400,000 micro-USD of uncommitted capacity must be available when no other image
owes review: the full image reservation and 250,000 for the ordinary assessment.
Each other already painted appearance that still owes its current ordinary
subject assessment requires another 250,000 of coverage before a new painting.
This includes review debt across boards; a copied pass for older pixels does
not clear it. A missing enrolled photograph refuses dispatch. Existing paid replays do
not need this new capacity. These are conservative operational allowances,
not a provider invoice or a proven absolute bound for arbitrary inputs.
UNKNOWN retains the entire image reservation; a missing response, missing usage
or unexpected returned model never means a zero charge. The world ceiling remains
4,000,000 micro-USD and cannot grow automatically.

When this coverage defers another Sunburst painting on a partial board, its paid
pictures get an ordinary-review turn first. This wake requires new pixels in the
current slice or a decrease in review debt; an unchanged permanently blocked
review parks instead of waking itself repeatedly. Historical painter scheduling
is unchanged.

At 768x1152, the official output calculator estimates 1,108 output tokens for
Sunburst HIGH and for the prior GPT Image 2 MEDIUM: $0.03324 output-only.
Sunburst MEDIUM is not selected. Image/text input, review, repair and unresolved
billing are additional. There is no measured new-model success rate or completed
real 27-hide Sunburst game under $4 in this change. Mock completion is a technical
check only. Health explicitly reports `realFindMeQualityMeasured:false` when the
profile is configured.
